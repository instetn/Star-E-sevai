FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a government office?
No. Star eSevai (Star Consultancy) is a private service facilitator. We assist you in preparing and submitting applications on official government portals; approval always rests with the respective department.
What do your charges include?
Our price includes a service/facilitation fee plus any government portal fee applicable to your application. The full amount is confirmed with you before we start.
How long does processing take?
Online services typically take same day to 3 working days from our side. Final approval time depends on the government department concerned.
Can I get a refund if I cancel?
Cancellation before we begin processing is usually possible. Once submission has started, government portal fees are non-refundable. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.
How do I receive my documents?
Digital copies by email/WhatsApp, physical collection at our office, or courier anywhere in India on request (charges apply).
