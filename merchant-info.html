Business Nature

We provide facilitation services for online government processes (ID documents, certificates, application filings) and offline services (printing, designing, binding). Customers pay us a service fee and, where required, we pay mandated portal fees or collect them on the customer's behalf.

Services & Examples of Transactions

  • Online application assistance (Aadhaar update, PAN, GST enrolment) — service charge plus applicable government portal fees
  • Exam application processing (NEET / JEE / TNPSC) — one-time per-application service fee
  • Printing & binding — fixed price per job
  • Police verification assistance and vehicle RC services — service-based pricing

Payment Flow

  1. Customer selects a service on the website or contacts us.
  2. We confirm the final price (service fee + portal fee if applicable) and send a payment link.
  3. Customer pays via Razorpay — payment captured in the merchant account.
  4. We begin the service; receipts / invoices are provided by email.
